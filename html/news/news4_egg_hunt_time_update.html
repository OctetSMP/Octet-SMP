Greetings, Egg Hunters!

The Ender Dragon's egg is up for grabs, and it's every player for themselves!

Welcome to The Egg Hunt, where the world is your battlefield, and the only prize that matters is that shimmering purple egg.

HOW THE HUNT WORKS:

• The Scramble: Everyone starts in random locations. Gather resources, build your base, and prepare for the hunt.

• The Waiting Game: The End is completely off-limits until the final day.

• The Final Hunt: On the last day, the End opens for everyone at the same time (20/8, The exact time will be announced later). All hunters rush in, defeat the Ender Dragon, and fight over who gets to snatch the egg.

• Win Condition: The hunter who secures the egg by unlocking the advancement "The Next Generation" is crowned the winner. It's not about who kills the dragon—it's about who keeps the egg.

RULES:

1. All's Fair: Griefing, PvP, and stealing are fully allowed. Lock your chests and watch your back!

2. Temporary Alliances: Cross-teaming is allowed. Team up to take down the dragon, but remember—when the egg drops, every hunter turns into a rival. Betrayal is part of the game.

3. No Early Birds: Entering the End before the scheduled final day = instant ban. This is your only warning.

4. Discord Share Screen: Hunters must share their screen in Discord to prevent X-Ray or any other kind of cheating. Please note that an Anti-Cheat system is now running on the server.
